Key Findings · Benchmark Insights

Performance Boundaries

Near-saturation on diagnostic tasks, but significant challenges remain for prognosis and treatment response.

Generalization Gap

External validation exposes substantial performance drops, highlighting limited cross-center robustness.

Architectural Impact

Patch-level models outperform WSI-level pipelines. IHC-aware pretraining provides clear molecular advantages.
